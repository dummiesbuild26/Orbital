ORBITAL · Privacy Policy

Effective 1 October 2026

ORBITAL is a casual arcade game. We collect as little as possible: no accounts, no names or email addresses, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

  • Your best score, sound setting and generated pilot name.
  • A random player ID created by the game (not linked to you, your device or your Apple ID).

What is sent to our game server

Only when you use online features (Quick Match, rooms with friends, global ranks):

  • Your random player ID and generated pilot name (for example "NeonComet").
  • Your scores, to show the global leaderboard.
  • Live match events (when you switch orbit or crash), relayed to the other players in your match and not stored after the match.

Pilot names are chosen by the game from a fixed word list; players cannot type messages or names, so no other player can send you text.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect personal information, contacts, location, photos or device identifiers.
  • We don't track you across apps or websites and don't sell or share data with anyone.
  • We don't use advertising or third-party analytics.

Where data is stored

The game server runs on Cloudflare Workers. Leaderboard entries (player ID, pilot name, best score, time) are kept until you delete them. Like any web service, Cloudflare processes IP addresses to deliver traffic; we don't store them.

Deleting your data

In the game, open Ranks and tap Delete my online data to remove your leaderboard entry immediately. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device. You can also email us (below) and we'll delete it for you.

Children

ORBITAL doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.
