ORBITAL · Support

How to play

  • Your comet circles the core. Dodge the spinning saws and grab gems to build your combo.
  • With 2 orbits, tap anywhere to switch orbit.
  • From level 3 there are more orbits: swipe up to move out one orbit and swipe down to move in toward the core. The game shows a short tutorial the first time.

Multiplayer

  • Quick Match races you against other players on the same course; the last comet alive wins. If nobody is online, tap Play vs Bots.
  • Friends: create a room and share the 4-letter code or invite link.

Common questions

No sound? Check the speaker button in the top-right corner and your silent switch.

Change my pilot name? Tap your name in the Friends or room screen to get a new one.

Remove my scores? Ranks → Delete my online data. See the privacy policy.
